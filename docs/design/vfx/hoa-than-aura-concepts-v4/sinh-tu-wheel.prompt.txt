Use case: game-asset concept exploration. Brand NEW aura-only transparent ART for a xianxia MMORPG, shown behind an unchanged native pixel chibi (character frame 64x96, body 45x80, foot 32,88). Do not draw any character.
Composition: one coherent aura backplate with a strong distinctive shape above and to the sides, plus a low horizontal ground ellipse near the bottom. Portrait canvas about 4:5. Leave the CENTRAL BODY CORRIDOR largely transparent: width about 40% of plate, running from about 40% height to the ground ellipse centre at about 90% height. The upper halo may close above the head. Ground ellipse centre is the single ground attachment point. Entire effect within canvas, 5% alpha margins. Painterly stylized game effect, simplified broad readable shapes, NOT detailed realistic smoke. Must read when the whole plate is reduced to roughly128x160 native pixels. CRITICAL visual contrast on a PALE beige stone game map: use a dense deep ink/indigo supporting edge and saturated medium color body behind bright narrow cores, not white/green mist alone. Strong alpha on designed bands; soft halo only near their edges. Stable crisp silhouette; preserve true transparent background, no opaque black backdrop or checkerboard.
NO person, face, costume, anatomy, weapon held by a person, text, kanji, UI labels, border, panel, watermark, or copied game logo. Do not paint the entire centre solid, do not obscure the imagined character. Not wings or two symmetrical smoke columns.
Design A — LIFE/DEATH DAO WHEEL. A broken vertical circular dao halo, slightly flattened in depth, with two interlocking currents. One side warm luminous ivory-gold life current; opposing side deep ink-navy death current edged in tiny silver-gold sparks. Dense dark-blue under-edge supports the entire ring so the gold core stands off a bright floor. A few broad geometric seals/segment marks around the ring, abstract and sparse, not readable words or a yin-yang logo. Cloud-curve tips at 2 or3 endpoints, calm powerful divine Soul Formation. A compact dark-ink ground ellipse edged gold, echoing the same two currents. No green smoke, no giant explosion, no all-white ring. The prominent dao wheel is behind the shoulders; empty centre preserves the full body.
