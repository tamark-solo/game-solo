Use case: illustration-story game animation asset.
One transparent pixel-art animation sheet for a top-down three-quarter cultivation H5 MMORPG. One boss identity only: Tran Son Khoi Loi, a stocky ancient stone guardian with a stern faceted stone mask instead of a human face, broad angular grey-blue slate shoulders, thick stone arms, short articulated stone legs, a few worn bronze joints and tiny muted jade eyes. Strong readable silhouette, restrained details, no giant ornament halo. Its native body is about 80 pixels wide and 104 high, only 1.3 times the height of an 80-pixel chibi player. Matching chunky limited-palette pixel sprite style, not photorealistic/painterly/3D.
Exactly 6 COLUMNS x 8 ROWS = 48 distinct full-body poses on ONE sheet, all facing SOUTH toward screen bottom. Fixed camera and identical art scale, mask shape, plate layout, root and floor in every cell. Large fully transparent gaps around all poses; no overlaps. Kneeling/dead poses remain SMALLER in height, not zoomed to fill the cell.
Rows, left-to-right temporal order:
1: idle, 6 restrained breathing/weight poses.
2: locomotion, 6 heavy alternating steps, arm counter-swing.
3: melee strike, 6 phases ready / draw stone arm back / clear raised-arm anticipation / downward strike / recovery / ready.
4: hit reaction, 6 small stone torso recoil/settle poses, no stun fall.
5: death, 6 weaken / knees buckle / kneel / slump / collapse / final fallen body. No explosion cloud.
6: appearance, 6 low dormant crouch / wake / rise / unfold arms / straighten / ready. Same floor/root.
7: line-slam skill gesture, 6 ready / brace / both hands lift / hands strike ground / settle / ready. No line, magic or hit effect baked in.
8: seal-field skill gesture, 6 ready / hands inward / hands open raised / firm casting stance / lower arms / ready. No ring or aura baked in.
True alpha transparency, crisp pixel clusters, clear material differences. No labels, numbers, grid, background, ground shadow, particles, bloom, flames, text, watermark or separate props. Bodies only: every skill effect will be attached as independent runtime layers later.
